How we treat roofs

Open air: Outdoors, so a verified pregame forecast can affect the research simulation. Fixed roof: Indoor, domed, or covered/skylight venue; the experimental model assumes no outdoor weather impact. Retractable: The structure can open or close; without a timestamped game-day roof announcement, V10+Weather is withheld.

This is a reference for each team's usual home stadium, not proof of a particular game venue. Neutral-site games, relocation, stadium changes, and retractable roof movement require separate verification. SoFi has a fixed canopy with open sides; local field conditions may differ from enclosed domes and remain a research limitation.
